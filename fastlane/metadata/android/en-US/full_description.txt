Focus Timer is a quiet timer for doing one thing at a time. Pick what you are working on, start the clock and put the phone down. When the time is up, a gentle alert fades in.

<ul>
<li>Save timers for the things you do often and start them in one tap</li>
<li>Run a one-time timer when you don't need to keep it</li>
<li>A full-screen ring that shows how long is left, and nothing else</li>
<li>An alert that fades in: built-in sounds or an alarm tone from your phone</li>
<li>Soft vibration patterns, and sound settings for each timer</li>
<li>A simple stats screen, kept on your device</li>
</ul>

No account, no ads and no tracking. The app has no internet permission, so your timers and stats never leave your phone.
